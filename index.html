Power Market Clearing

Five generation technologies bid at their marginal cost, forming the supply merit order. Four demand blocks bid their willingness to pay — Firm demand is inelastic (must-serve) and always served first. The market clears where the two curves cross — that intersection sets both the clearing price and the traded volume. If generation capacity falls short of even firm demand, firm load must be curtailed and price jumps to firm demand's own marginal value.

Supply & demand curves

Merit order (cheapest first) meets demand willingness-to-pay (highest first). Shaded areas show consumer surplus (value to buyers above the price paid) and producer surplus (revenue to sellers above their cost).
